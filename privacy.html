Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

My Oura Export is a personal tool used by its owner to download and analyze their own Oura data.

The tool accesses sleep, readiness, activity, and heart-rate data with the owner's authorization through Oura.

The exporter runs on the owner's computer. Downloaded health data is saved locally. Application credentials and access tokens are held in memory during each run and are not intentionally written to files or logs.

This website does not receive Oura health data or credentials. The tool does not sell data or send it to advertising or analytics services. Any later sharing of exported files is controlled by the owner.

The owner can delete downloaded files from their computer and revoke the application's access through their Oura account.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information about visits under its own Privacy Statement.

This tool is for the owner's personal use and is not offered as a service to other users.
